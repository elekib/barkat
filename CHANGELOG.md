# Changelog

Follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Before 1.0.0 a minor bump may include breaking changes.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Insights at `/s/<book>/insights`, with a gradient banner that rises in at the top of the month view (headline, count of things to watch, "See insights" button), a larger banner on the Overview page that rolls up every book (count of findings, the top ones each linking to their book's insights, and the typical monthly change across books with the likely cushion range), and a link in the year header: found from the book's own history with plain statistics (no model, nothing leaves the server). It shows the typical month (income less spending over the last 6 months), a 3-month cushion forecast with a low-to-high range, and flags for the latest month: a bill far from its usual amount, a regular bill that is missing, a new bill, a spending spike, and a cushion forecast to go negative. Needs 3 months with data. Adjustment entries and items set to None are left out.
- Clear future months: a month started ahead (by a schedule or copy-forward) offers "Clear <month>" in the month picker, so one can be deleted at a time. Only months after the current one can be cleared, since removing an earlier month would change the balances that carry forward. A dialog confirms first. To remove them all at once, the book's Import and export page has "Clear N future months", with a list of what goes. Admins only; the current and earlier months and the income schedules are untouched, and a cleared month can be started again.
- Screenshots: nine screens in `docs/screenshots/` (overview, a month, accounts and bills, Manage accounts, the month picker, the year view, import and export, the light theme and a phone) shown in the README. All are taken from the made-up demo data.
- Demo data: `npm run seed:demo` (in `web/`) writes made-up books to `web/data-demo/` with monthly history from January 2018 to the current month (about 170 months in two books: accounts, bills with yearly price rises, seasonal electricity, yearly zakat, trips, paychecks, a tax refund, one admin override and an income schedule). It never touches `web/data`, leaves an existing demo database alone unless `SEED_RESET=1`, and adds two fixed demo logins if missing (`demo`, admin, and `viewer`, read-only; password in the README), so the demo needs no setup screen.
- Month picker: the month in the pinned header is a button that opens a calendar of years and months. Each month says in words (and a dot, not colour alone) whether it is started, empty or not started, the current month is marked "Now", and the month on screen is outlined. A month that is not started opens the usual "start this month" card. It works from the keyboard (arrow keys move between months, PageUp and PageDown change the year, Escape closes), as a dialog sized for a phone, and offers every year from the book's first month to now.
- Year view at `/s/<book>/year/<yyyy>`: for each month the income, spending, saved (income less planned spending) and end-of-month cushion, with year totals, the average month, the savings rate, a chart of income and spending (each month links to its page) and a month-by-month table with the same numbers. Months with no data are listed and left out of the totals and averages; a year with no data says so and links to the years that have some. The cushion leaves out admin overrides, like the trend, and income and spending leave out "Adjustment" entries (corrections that make balances meet, such as an import's join), which still count in the cushion. Linked from the month picker and from a year button beside the month arrows. The year heading is a picker like the month heading: select it for a grid of years, each with its month count.
- Import many files, and older years: Import and export takes several Excel workbooks and Barkat backups (.json) at once. Each workbook has its own year (filled in from the file name when it has one) for sheets named only "March". "Preview" lists every month (bills, one-offs, income, accounts, and whether it is added, replaced or skipped) and every sheet that is not a month, writing nothing; "Import" then does exactly that, in one step, oldest month first. A month in two files is taken from the first. A backup restores its months as they were, including balances and admin overrides.
- Months added in front of existing ones never change them. Balances carry from month to month, so a 2022-2025 import in front of 2026 would have moved every 2026 balance. Instead, each existing month that now follows a new month gets its old opening back with an "Adjustment" entry (income for a rise, a one-off for a fall) in the new month just before it, never an edit to the existing month, so its balances, cushion, entries and admin overrides stay exactly as they were. The preview lists the adjustments. The same holds when a month is started or copied into in front of every other month.
- Project scaffold: Next.js app, Docker image, compose files, CI.
- Budget core: the spreadsheet's bill, income and cushion formulas as tested pure functions.
- Ad-hoc payments: one-off money out (principal-only mortgage payments, zakat, trips) that fall out of the same cushion and account as bills, with tags.
- SQLite database (`data/barkat.db`) with versioned migrations; spaces, months, banks, bills, income and one-off payments.
- Weekly screen per space and month: summary cards, editable rows that save as you go, status colours.
- New month: copies bills, bank balances and weekly income from the previous month, resets statuses, leaves one-offs behind.
- Overview page: greeting header, totals across all books (cushion, in the bank, still to pay, income to arrive), richer book cards (paid meter, due status, accounts) and a "Worth a look" list (late or soon-due bills, negative cushion, months not started); "Create book" is a button with a dialog. Spaces are now called books in the app. Books can be deleted from the Overview page: a dialog lists what will be lost and asks for the book's name to be typed. Books can be reordered (a Reorder dialog); the order sets the tabs, the cards and which "Worth a look" items come first.
- Glass cards in both themes (a diagonal sheen and a lighter rim) on every card, dialog, menu and chart tooltip; accessibility pass: text and status colours checked for contrast on the sheen, per-fill text colours on the status bar, larger tap targets, and reduced-motion support.
- Tables: every editable table (accounts, bills, one-offs, income, income schedules) has row select boxes and a select-all. A toolbar acts on the selection: set status, set account, or delete (with a confirmation). The per-row delete cross is gone, so nothing overlaps the Due column. The By account table shares the same layout, rows are transparent on the card, and drop-downs have their own chevron with room on the right.
- Balances carry from month to month. An account's first month holds its opening balance; each later month opens with what the month before projected for it (its cushion) and then moves only by entries: income Received adds, bills and one-offs marked Paid or Payment Made subtract. A carried opening cannot be typed; to correct a balance, add an income or a one-off. Adding, renaming or deleting an account applies to it in every later month. Existing books are converted when the app starts: each account's first month keeps its typed balance as the opening, and a later month whose typed balance differs gets an "Adjustment" income or one-off, so no month's figures change. Importing a sheet does the same for the month it brings in.
- Accounts card: one card for the book's accounts (replacing By account and the month's Banks sheet): balance, change this month (▲ green / ▼ red), income due, bills, one-offs and cushion. A "Manage accounts" button opens a dialog where every account's name, type and balance (and a new account) are edited and saved together with one Save. A changed balance is an admin override: it is set as typed with no entry, later entries still move it, and later months carry on from it. Overrides and the starting balance of a new account are left out of trends (the "This month" change and the change since last month). Removing an account asks first and says how many entries lose their account; accounts are added, renamed and removed for this month and every later one.
- Full backup: Import and export has a "Download backup (.json)" that holds a whole book (every month, account with its opening balance and any admin override, bill, one-off, income entry and income schedule). Create book has "Restore as a new book", so a book can be recreated in another Barkat or alongside the original (a name already in use gets " (2)"). The file is checked before anything is written, and the restore is all or nothing. Users and settings are not part of it. The Excel export is unchanged.
- Back buttons: Import and export, Users and Account have a real bordered button with an arrow at the top ("Back to November 2026", "Back to Books") in place of a small text link.
- Dashboard: cushion hero, key-number tiles, charts (spending by status, money in and out, largest payments, one-offs by tag) and an income-versus-spending trend with hover details and a table view.
- Light and dark themes with an Auto / Light / Dark switch; restyled screens.
- Tailwind CSS for utility styling, driven by the theme tokens.
- Navigation bar (space tabs, user menu, mobile bottom bar).
- Authentication (Better Auth): first-run setup code, admin and viewer roles, Users and Account pages, session list, 7-day sessions, sign-in rate limit.
- Income schedules: weekly, every-two-weeks and monthly paydays per space. A new month fills its income from the schedules, and "Add paydays" fills the current month without duplicating. Monthly paydays on the 29th to 31st fall on the last day of shorter months.
- Excel import and export: each sheet is a month in the original layout (Expenses table, Income list, bank balances). Export writes one sheet per month with the sheet's formulas; import skips existing months unless told to replace them, and reports sheets it could not read. One-off payments are exported among the expenses with an extra Kind column so they round-trip.
- Accounts on every payment and income: choose which account a bill or one-off is paid from and which account income goes into (a schedule names its account too). A "By account" table splits the cushion per account, with the lines adding up to the total. Accounts carry into new months, and Excel import and export keep them (columns O and R; the import can give rows with no account to the first one, as the original single-account sheet implied).
- Copy from month: a button beside the month arrows opens a dialog. Pick any other month, choose to add what is missing or to replace the bills and accounts, preview every line, and untick any you do not want. Replace asks first; one-offs and income stay, re-linked by account name. A bill's account comes along with it. On a month that has not been started the same button is there too: the start card is the quick suggestion, and the button starts the month empty and fills it with just the lines you pick. The month header (name and arrows) stays pinned under the top bar on wider screens.
- Due badge in the pinned month header: red when a bill with a due day is past due and unpaid, amber within three days, neutral after that, with an icon and words as well as colour. Bills marked Payment Made, Paid or None stop counting.
- Visual polish: icon chips on every section, chart and tile title, icons on the add buttons, a soft accent glow on the cushion card, and a hover edge on tiles.
- Collapsible sections: every sheet, chart and the By account table folds from its heading. A folded sheet shows a one-line summary (for example the bills to pay), and the choice is remembered in the browser.
- Theme: a fresher light theme (soft sage page and cards with a gentle green glow at the top, softer ink and borders) and the original mint-on-black dark theme. The logo, favicon and app icons match.
- Logo and icons: a crescent-and-coin mark with a soft sparkle and faint starfield, horizontal and stacked logos for light and dark, favicon, app icons, web manifest and a social preview, generated by `branding/generate.mjs`.

### Changed
- Months load faster with many years of them: the whole book's balances are carried once instead of once per month, and the month page reads them once for both the copy sources and the trend. The "Over time" chart labels about a dozen months however many there are.
- The import action takes several files at once (up to 40, and up to 40 MB in all) and the preview/import button pair replaces the single Import button.

### Fixed
- The To pay and Still to arrive totals under the Bills, One-off payments and Income tables no longer move with the table when it is scrolled sideways. They now sit under the table at the edge of the card.
